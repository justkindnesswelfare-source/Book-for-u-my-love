CHAPTER 01

The Girl in the Library

She loved books because between their pages, the world became quiet. Every story was a door, every sentence a secret passage to somewhere new.

And somewhere in that endless library, a story was waiting that didn't begin with a prince or a perfect hero. It began with a simple thought: what if someone saw the world the way she did?

A page from my heart

Dear Rinku…

To the girl who loves stories,

Hey meri Rinku 🎀

Main soch raha tha ki tumhe aisa kya doon jo tumhari favourite duniya se juda ho. Phir laga, tumhe books pasand hain… toh kyun na tumhare liye ek chhoti si digital book banaun?

Tum meri life ki koi perfect fictional character nahi ho. Tum real ho — apni feelings, apni choices, apni khushiyon aur apne dreams ke saath. Aur shayad isi wajah se tum mere liye itni special ho.

Agar kabhi duniya bahut loud lage, toh apni favourite book khol lena. Aur agar kabhi tumhe lage ki koi tumhari baat sunne wala chahiye, toh main chahta hoon ki tum mujhse bina dare apne dil ki baat keh sako.

Main tumhari har kahani ka hero banne ka daawa nahi karta. Bas itna chahta hoon ki main tumhari life mein respect, honesty aur pyaar lekar aaun.

Keep reading, keep dreaming, aur apni kahani ki main character hamesha khud rehna. ❤️

Yours, RJ ♡
